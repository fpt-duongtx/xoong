Admin patterns

What the Rainlet admin asked for, in the shared Mist look so it can delete its own copies: a segmented group for any button[aria-pressed], the modal, the form rhythm, the 44px hit area and tables that become cards on a narrow pane. Classes are in css/mist.css.

Segmented group

Add class="xo-seg" to any group of button[aria-pressed]: the language switcher, the crop shapes and the preview scale. The language badge is .xo-seg__badge with its own glyph (never colour alone); [hidden] always wins.

Modal

dialog.xo-modal with .xo-modal__head (title and a close button), .xo-modal__body (scrolls) and .xo-modal__foot (actions, primary last). --wide and --full widen it; under 768px it is full screen.

Choose an image

Form rhythm

20px between fields, 6px from label to control and from control to hint or error; tab panels follow the same rhythm. Put .xo-fields (or .xo-form) on the block; a prose form keeps .xo-prose (720px).

Shown in the sitemap and the browser tab.
Enter a full e-mail address.

Icon-only buttons: 40px, 44px hit area

The button is 40px (32px small); an invisible 44px target (36/44 around the small one) stays inside the row. The dashed outline shows the target.

Tables on a narrow pane: row cards

Under 768px (of the table box) the header hides and every row becomes a card: title and status on the first line, labelled values below, actions last; selected rows keep the tint and the 3px bar. Every td needs data-label; data-title marks the title cell and data-status the status cell.
