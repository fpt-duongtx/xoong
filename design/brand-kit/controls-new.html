New controls

The controls xoong does not have yet, in the Rainlet (Mist) look: UI only, static. The dev side builds the elements, keyboard, ARIA and form values; the look must not change. Proposed element names and behaviour are in CONTROLS-SPEC.md.

Buttons beyond the basics: group, split, toggle, busy, link

A button group picks one of a few icon options. A split button has a main action and a menu. A toggle button (aria-pressed) keeps a tool on. Busy shows a spinner; the label stays.

Scrollbars: thumb only

The track and the corner are transparent; only the thumb is drawn (6px inside a 12px hit area, 40px minimum). Put xo-scroll on any scroll area.
