Controls

The controls every admin needs, as custom elements in the Light DOM with plain CSS. @xoong/controls depends only on @xoong/core, so it works with or without the rest.

  • Tabs

    The WAI-ARIA tabs pattern; panels stay in the form.

    <xo-tabs>

  • Menu and tooltip

    A menu button in the top layer, choice menus, descriptions on hover and focus.

    <xo-menu> · xo-tooltip

  • Combobox and tags

    Pick from a list you can filter, or type free tags. Both are form controls.

    <xo-combobox> · <xo-tags>

  • Carousel

    Cards, banners, testimonials, galleries, on the platform's scroll snap.

    <xo-carousel>

  • Rich text

    An accessible toolbar over a contenteditable area; the value is sanitised HTML.

    <xo-richtext>

  • Your own control

    A form control of your own, built on XoControl.

    XoControl

  • Standard

    Sizes, states and fields every control shares.

    controls.css

  • All controls

    Every control on one page, in the demo frame.

    demo
