# Code of conduct

We want a project where people can disagree about design and still be treated with respect.

* Be kind and patient. Assume good faith; ask before you assume otherwise.
* Criticise code, designs and ideas, never the person. Be specific and offer an alternative where you can.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
* Keep issues and reviews on topic, and credit other people's work.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may restrict participation of people who do so
repeatedly. To report a problem, use a private channel to a maintainer (the repository's Security tab works for this too,
until a dedicated address exists).
