Loading, empty and error

What the people table shows while it loads, when there is nothing to show, and when the request fails. Each state says what happened and offers one next step.

Loading

A skeleton the shape of the table: header bar and five rows, shimmering. It is announced once as Loading and drawn only while $loading is true; reduced motion stops the shimmer.

Copied
Loading people

Empty

The empty slot of the table. The pot has its lid off. The primary action is the thing that fills the table.

Copied

Nobody here yet

No one matches this view. Clear the search, or add the first person.

Error

Shown with $error. Red outline and title, the problem code and detail from the response, one button that refetches. role="alert" announces it.

Copied

Could not load people

NETWORK Failed to fetch. Check your connection and try again.

The markup

All three states in one view, as the page wires them.
