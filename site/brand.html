Brand kit

The identity of xoong: a small pot with a lot of heat.

The idea

Xoong is the Vietnamese word for a saucepan, the pot every kitchen has and nobody replaces. That is the framework: one small, sturdy tool that does the whole job. The steam rising from the lid is a pair of angle brackets, so the pot reads as code at a glance.

Personality: practical, warm, a little cheeky. We talk like a good cook explaining a recipe, not like a vendor selling a platform.

Logo

Mark and wordmark

The mark is the only symbol. The wordmark is lowercase xoong, heavy weight, tracking −0.045em. On this site the mark is inline SVG and follows the palette (use the menu in the header); the files below are fixed colour.

Colour

Two palettes, one structure.

Calm (clay and sage on warm paper) is the default. Ember turns the heat up. A palette is only a set of custom-property values, so a third is easy: see the tabs below.

  • Clay#DF8A6A

    Calm: the pot, fills.

  • Clay Deep#9A4D33

    Calm: links, primary actions.

  • Ink#2B2623

    Text, outlines, dark surfaces.

  • Paper#F7F4EC

    Page background.

  • Ember#FF5B22

    Ember palette: the pot.

  • Ember Deep#B93A0A

    Ember: links on light.

  • Sesame#E6C783

    Notes, highlights.

Voice

Say it like a cook.

Do
  • Be concrete: “one way to do each thing”.
  • Use kitchen words lightly: pot, recipe, ingredients.
  • Say the status honestly: prototype, Draft syntax.
  • Lead with what the reader can do next.
Don't
  • Say “revolutionary”, “blazing” or “next-gen”.
  • Use more than one cooking word per paragraph.
  • Promise what is still planned.
  • Write the name in capitals. It is xoong.
